%% ------------------------------------------------------ 1
\heading{1.}{Problem}

Official documents are falsified through edited text, replaced photos, forged
stamps and altered identifiers --- and now through AI generation. Manual review
is slow, repetitive, and misses precisely the subtle cases. The reviewer rarely
lacks judgement: they lack time, and a place to look.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 2
\heading{2.}{Target users}

\lead{User} The person who checks documents at volume: a credit analyst, an HR
recruiter, a compliance officer, an administrative clerk.
\lead{Buyer} The head of risk, compliance or operations, who buys reduced
exposure rather than saved minutes.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 3
\heading{3.}{Solution --- screening, not certification}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{note}{What the product claims, and what it refuses to claim}
DocShield AI does not certify documents. It surfaces suspicious signals and the
evidence behind them, so a human reviewer decides faster and better: every flag
is drawn and numbered on the document itself. A document that is fabricated but
internally coherent passes every check here --- screening detects inconsistency,
not falsity, and authenticity can only be established with the issuer.
\end{note}

\vspace{4pt}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{goodbox}{Handled by the model}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt,itemsep=3pt,topsep=1pt,
                label=\textcolor{good}{\small$\checkmark$}]
  \item \textbf{Reads} the document and extracts fields.
  \item \textbf{Sees} visual manipulation: mixed fonts, broken alignment,
        re-inserted text.
  \item \textbf{Compares} pages against one another.
\end{itemize}
\end{goodbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{badbox}{Never trusted to the model}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt,itemsep=3pt,topsep=1pt,
                label=\textcolor{bad}{\small$\times$}]
  \item \textbf{Counting digits} --- identifier length.
  \item \textbf{Checksums} --- IBAN mod-97 key.
  \item \textbf{Arithmetic and calendars} --- VAT, totals, impossible dates,
        inverted periods.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize\color{muted}Deterministic code: exact and auditable.}
\end{badbox}
\end{minipage}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 4
\heading{4.}{Prototype --- deployed and open}

{\footnotesize\color{steel}\bfseries
Upload $\rightarrow$ Normalization $\rightarrow$ Multimodal analysis
$\rightarrow$ Deterministic checks $\rightarrow$ Risk score $\rightarrow$ \mbox{Report}}

\vspace{3pt}
PDF, JPG and PNG. Every page is rendered and sent together, so a value changing
between two pages is visible to the model. Preview and model input share the
same images, so a flagged region lands where the reviewer looks. Output passes a
strict JSON schema, re-validated server-side; a failed deterministic check
raises the score floor and blocks an ``accept'' verdict.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 5
\heading{5.}{Validation --- the loop that shaped the build}

{\footnotesize\color{steel}\bfseries
Build $\rightarrow$ Test $\rightarrow$ Failure $\rightarrow$ Diagnose
$\rightarrow$ Fix $\rightarrow$ Retest $\rightarrow$ Learn}

\vspace{3pt}
Five reviewers from five companies: four ran documents through the deployed
prototype, one assessed the product without testing it. The first reported four
defects, each
traced to a specific line before anything was changed, all four corrected within
the challenge window. Deliverable 03 records every message verbatim, failures
included.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 6
\heading{6.}{Key learnings --- and what each one is worth}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{30mm}
                  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{\dimexpr\linewidth-37mm}@{}}
\rowcolor{zebra}\textbf{\color{good}PROVEN} &
A planted date inconsistency detected, named and localised on a document
designed by a third party. Genuine certified documents passed unflagged. \\[2pt]
\textbf{\color{navy}FIXED} &
Four reported defects corrected and deployed; two independently observed
working afterwards. \\[2pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}\textbf{\color{bad}TO RETEST} &
Cross-page fix deployed and pipeline verified, but the two-page invoice that
exposed the gap has not been re-run --- end-to-end detection not yet
demonstrated. \\[2pt]
\textbf{\color{bad}LIMIT} &
A well-made or AI-generated document that is internally coherent is missed by
design. \\
\end{tabular}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 7
\heading{7.}{Business logic}

\lead{Value} Less manual screening, faster triage, a traceable record of every
check. Minutes saved and the cost of a missed forgery are \emph{not} measured
yet, and no figure is asserted here.\\
\lead{Model \small\color{bad}(hypothesis)} Per document, per seat, or an
integration API --- to be tested in pricing conversations, not assumed.\\
\lead{Beachhead \small\color{bad}(hypothesis)} Lending and leasing in Morocco,
for volume and direct loss per miss. Two reviewers pointed instead to public
administration and education; the question is open.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 8
\heading{8.}{Next step}

Reliability before reach. Measure false positives and false negatives on a
controlled document set, re-run the outstanding test, then pilot with one
organization before adding any new detection category.

\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize\color{muted}\textbf{Key assumption.} Organizations will adopt
AI-assisted screening only if every flag carries transparent evidence. An
unjustified score will not be trusted --- and should not be.}
