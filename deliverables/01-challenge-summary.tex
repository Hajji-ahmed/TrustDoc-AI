%% ------------------------------------------------------ 1
\heading{1.}{Problem and users}

Official documents are falsified through edited text, replaced photos, forged
stamps, altered QR codes --- and now through AI generation. Manual review is
slow, repetitive, and misses precisely the subtle cases.

\lead{Who.} Teams that verify documents at volume: recruitment, finance,
compliance and administration.

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{note}{Positioning}
An AI-assisted screening tool that returns \textbf{evidence a reviewer can act
on} --- not a verdict. Every flag is drawn and numbered on the document itself,
so the reviewer sees \emph{where} to look, not only \emph{that} something is wrong.
\end{note}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 2
\heading{2.}{Evidence}

\begin{note}{Tests run against the working prototype}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textcolor{navy}{\bfseries\arabic*.}]
  \item AI-generated invoice, visually flawless $\rightarrow$ scored \emph{low risk}.
  \item Client tax identifier of invalid length $\rightarrow$ missed by the model.
  \item Low score returned together with a reject verdict $\rightarrow$ contradictory output.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{2pt}
Each finding changed the build. An external tester at TecForge then ran three
documents through the prototype and reported four further defects --- all
corrected within the challenge window. See deliverable 03.
\end{note}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 3
\heading{3.}{Solution --- a division of labour}

{\footnotesize\color{steel}\bfseries
Upload $\rightarrow$ Normalization $\rightarrow$ Multimodal analysis
$\rightarrow$ Deterministic checks $\rightarrow$ Risk score $\rightarrow$ \mbox{Report}}

\vspace{5pt}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{goodbox}{Handled by the model}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt,itemsep=4pt,topsep=1pt,
                label=\textcolor{good}{\small$\checkmark$}]
  \item \textbf{Reads} the document and extracts fields.
  \item \textbf{Sees} visual manipulation: mixed fonts, broken alignment,
        re-inserted text.
  \item \textbf{Explains} each finding in plain language.
\end{itemize}
\end{goodbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{badbox}{Never trusted to the model}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt,itemsep=4pt,topsep=1pt,
                label=\textcolor{bad}{\small$\times$}]
  \item \textbf{Counting digits} --- identifier length.
  \item \textbf{Checksums} --- IBAN mod-97 key.
  \item \textbf{Arithmetic} --- VAT and column totals.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{3pt}
{\footnotesize\color{muted}Handled by deterministic code: exact and auditable.}
\end{badbox}
\end{minipage}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 4
\heading{4.}{Prototype --- live}

\begin{itemize}
  \item PDF, JPG, PNG. Every page is rendered and sent, so an inconsistency
        between two pages of one document is visible to the model. Preview and
        model input share the same images, so a flagged region always lands
        where the reviewer looks.
  \item Strict JSON schema, re-validated server-side --- a malformed analysis is
        rejected, never half-displayed.
  \item A failed deterministic check raises the score and blocks an ``accept''
        verdict.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize\color{muted}
\textbf{Out of 72h scope}, part of the target architecture: OCR tuning,
dedicated manipulation models, metadata forensics, cross-document checks.}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 5
\heading{5.}{Validation and business logic}

Focus: how documents are verified today, which frauds are hardest to catch, and
what evidence earns a reviewer's trust. A learning log records who was
contacted, what was learned, what was surprising, and what changed.

\vspace{2pt}
\lead{Customer} \ Organizations verifying documents at volume --- lenders,
insurers, recruiters, schools, administrations.\\
\lead{Value} \ Less manual screening, faster triage, traceable evidence.\\
\lead{Model} \ B2B SaaS per seat or per document, plus an integration API.\\
\lead{Market} \ Finance, recruitment, insurance, education, public sector.\\
\lead{Next 30 days} \ Pilot with one organization, add metadata forensics and
cross-document consistency, test pricing.

\vspace{7pt}
\begin{note}{Key assumption}
Organizations will adopt AI-assisted screening only if it shows transparent
evidence for every flag. A score without justification will not be trusted ---
and should not be.
\end{note}
