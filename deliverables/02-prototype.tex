\vspace{2pt}
Upload a PDF, JPG or PNG, run the analysis, and receive an authenticity
pre-check: a risk score, anomalies drawn on the document itself, and a
recommendation. The interface states plainly that the result is decision
support --- not an expertise, not a proof.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 2
\heading{2.}{Pipeline}

{\footnotesize\color{steel}\bfseries
Upload $\rightarrow$ Normalization $\rightarrow$ Multimodal analysis
$\rightarrow$ Deterministic checks $\rightarrow$ Risk score $\rightarrow$ \mbox{Report}}

\vspace{4pt}
% Les 52mm retranchés couvrent les deux colonnes fixes (43mm) et les quatre
% \tabcolsep des gouttières internes, que la largeur des colonnes ignore.
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{9mm}
                  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{34mm}
                  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{\dimexpr\linewidth-52mm}@{}}
\rowcolor{zebra}\textbf{\color{navy}01} & \textbf{Upload} &
PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10\,MB. Every page of a PDF is rendered, not only the
first. \\[3pt]
\textbf{\color{navy}02} & \textbf{Normalization} &
Pages are rasterized client-side. Preview and model input are the same image,
so a flagged region always lands where the reviewer looks. \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}\textbf{\color{navy}03} & \textbf{Multimodal analysis} &
All pages go to the model in a single request, which lets it compare values
across pages instead of judging each page alone. \\[3pt]
\textbf{\color{navy}04} & \textbf{Deterministic checks} &
Code re-verifies what a model cannot be trusted with: identifier length,
IBAN mod-97 key, VAT arithmetic, ordering of dates. \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}\textbf{\color{navy}05} & \textbf{Report} &
Risk score out of 100, numbered anomalies, extracted fields, and a
recommendation oriented toward human verification. \\
\end{tabular}

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 3
\heading{3.}{Division of labour --- what the model is never asked to do}

\vspace{3pt}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{goodbox}{Handled by the model}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt,itemsep=3pt,topsep=1pt,
                label=\textcolor{good}{\small$\checkmark$}]
  \item \textbf{Reads} the document and extracts fields.
  \item \textbf{Sees} visual manipulation: mixed fonts, broken alignment,
        re-inserted text.
  \item \textbf{Compares} pages against one another.
\end{itemize}
\end{goodbox}
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\linewidth}
\begin{badbox}{Never trusted to the model}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=14pt,itemsep=3pt,topsep=1pt,
                label=\textcolor{bad}{\small$\times$}]
  \item \textbf{Counting digits} --- identifier length.
  \item \textbf{Checksums} --- IBAN mod-97 key.
  \item \textbf{Arithmetic} --- VAT and column totals.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize\color{muted}Deterministic code: exact, and auditable line by line.}
\end{badbox}
\end{minipage}

\vspace{5pt}
A failed deterministic check raises the score floor and blocks an ``accept''
verdict. A model that saw nothing cannot overrule arithmetic that does not add up.

%% ------------------------------------------------------ 4
\heading{4.}{What the repository contains}

\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{32mm}
                  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{\dimexpr\linewidth-39mm}@{}}
\rowcolor{zebra}\texttt{app/api/analyze} &
Analysis endpoint. Validates the request, calls the model, re-validates the
output, then consolidates it. \\[3pt]
\texttt{lib/prompt.ts} &
The system prompt: inspection method, score calibration, field-naming rules,
and the ban on verifying a document against its own contact details. \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}\texttt{lib/schema.ts} &
Strict JSON schema. A malformed analysis is rejected server-side, never
half-displayed. \\[3pt]
\texttt{lib/validators.ts} &
The deterministic checks and the consolidation that keeps score, verdict and
narrative from contradicting one another. \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}\texttt{lib/pdf.ts} &
Multi-page rasterization, with the page cap that bounds cost per analysis. \\
\end{tabular}

\heading{6.}{Positioning and honest limits}

DocShield AI is a screening aid, not an authentication authority. The interface
frames every output as decision support and recommends human verification when
the evidence warrants it.

\vspace{2pt}
{\footnotesize\color{muted}
\textbf{Out of 72h scope}, part of the target architecture: OCR tuning,
dedicated manipulation models, metadata forensics, cross-document checks.}
