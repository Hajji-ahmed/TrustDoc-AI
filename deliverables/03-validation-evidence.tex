%% ------------------------------------------------------ 2
\heading{2.}{The four defects, and their cause in the code}

Each report was traced to a specific line before anything was changed. None was
a tuning problem; all four were logic errors.

\vspace{3pt}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textcolor{navy}{\bfseries\arabic*.}]
  \item \fld{A genuine ISO certificate scored 50/100.}
        The IBAN checksum fired on the shape of a value while ignoring its
        label. ``Two letters, two digits, then 11 to 30 characters'' describes a
        certificate number as well as a bank account, so the mod-97 key failed
        on an authentic document --- and a single failed check imposed a score
        floor of 50, turning a false positive into ``moderate risk''.

  \item \fld{The count said one problem, the summary said none.}
        The model writes its explanation before the deterministic checks run.
        It could therefore report ``nothing found'' while those checks were
        adding an anomaly to the list. Two true statements about two different
        stages, printed as one contradiction.

  \item \fld{An invoice number changed between page 1 and page 2 went unseen.}
        The PDF renderer read \texttt{getPage(1)} only, while reporting the real
        page count. The altered page never reached the model. The tester ranked
        this the most important defect; the diagnosis confirms it, since no
        amount of prompt tuning could have recovered a page that was never sent.

  \item \fld{``Contact the email on the invoice to verify.''}
        A forged document carries forged contact details. The prompt asked for
        ``concrete verification steps'' without ever forbidding the document
        from vouching for itself.
\end{enumerate}

\heading{3.}{From feedback to shipped change}

Every point raised was corrected and deployed within the challenge window, not
deferred to a backlog. The corresponding changes are public in the project
repository:
\href{https://github.com/Hajji-ahmed/TrustDoc-AI}{github.com/Hajji-ahmed/TrustDoc-AI}.

\vspace{3pt}
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{40mm}
                  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{\dimexpr\linewidth-73mm}
                  >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{22mm}@{}}
\rowcolor{zebra}\textbf{\color{navy}Reported} & \textbf{\color{navy}Change made} &
\textbf{\color{navy}Status} \\[3pt]
Wrong rule applied to a date &
The IBAN check now requires a label that names a bank account. An IBAN filed
under a label that does not say so escapes the check --- accusing a genuine
document costs more than missing one verification. & \shipped \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}Summary contradicts detail &
Each statement is attributed to its stage: visual analysis first, automatic
checks second. The model's prose is kept, not rewritten. & \shipped \\[3pt]
Two pages never compared &
All pages are rendered and sent to the model in one request, each announced by
its number. The prompt requires values repeated across pages to be compared. &
\shipped \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}Email alone is weak evidence &
The prompt forbids any verification routed through the document's own contact
details, and requires an independent source instead. & \shipped \\[3pt]
Field types left implicit \emph{(tester's suggestion)} &
The prompt now states which labels may be used for which value types, so the
wrong deterministic check cannot be triggered downstream. & \shipped \\[3pt]
\rowcolor{zebra}Cross-page detection on the tester's own file &
Route contract verified on six cases; the two-page invoice has not been run
again through the model. & \open \\
\end{tabular}

\vspace{5pt}
\begin{note}{What this round actually taught}
Extraction quality was never the weak point --- the tester confirmed it works,
including on flattened scans. Every defect he found sat in the layer that
decides \emph{what the reading means}: which rule applies to which field,
whether pages are compared, whether the narrative matches the findings. A
screening tool is not limited by what it can read, but by the discipline of what
it concludes.
\end{note}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{badbox}{The correction that has not yet been proven}
Points 1, 2 and 4 are verified by automated assertions. Point 3 --- the one the
tester ranked highest --- is verified only up to the request contract: all pages
now reach the model. Whether the model \emph{notices} the altered invoice number
requires re-running his own two-page file. Until that is done, this defect is
fixed in the pipeline and unproven in the result.
\end{badbox}

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{note}{Assessment}
\emph{``DocShield AI répond à un besoin réel de pré-contrôle documentaire.''}
The combination of AI analysis, confidence score, anomaly detection and

\end{note}

\shot{assets/feedback-managov.png}{Feedback received from Abdelkrim Zrouli (Managov), reproduced verbatim.}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{note}{The reservation, and why it matters more than the praise}
\emph{``Le principal enjeu sera de démontrer la fiabilité des résultats et de
bien distinguer détection d'anomalies et preuve d'authenticité.''}

\vspace{3pt}
This is the product's own founding constraint, stated back by someone who had
not been told it. DocShield never claims a document is authentic; it reports
what looks irregular and hands the decision to a human. Hearing that boundary
named as \emph{the} central challenge, by a director evaluating the tool for his
own organisation, confirms that the distinction is not academic --- it is what
buyers will judge the product on.
\end{note}

\vspace{4pt}
\heading{4.1}{The test design --- one controlled variable}

Both documents are byte-for-byte identical except for the validity window. That
is a cleaner experiment than anything run in-house: it isolates a single failure
mode and ships with its own control.

\vspace{3pt}

The planted defect is a period that ends before it begins, on a document issued
after both dates. It is obvious to a human and invisible to any check that reads
fields one at a time.

\heading{4.2}{The result --- the planted defect was caught}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{goodbox}{Document B, scored by the deployed prototype}
\fld{Score} 34/100 --- moderate risk. \fld{Type} Attestation administrative,
correctly identified.

\vspace{3pt}
\fld{Anomaly} One, and only one: \emph{``La période concernée commence en 2026
mais termine en 2025, ce qui est chronologiquement impossible.''} Classified
under data coherence, moderate severity, and drawn as a numbered region on the
document itself.

\vspace{3pt}
\fld{Recommendation} Manual verification required --- confirm the details with
the issuer \emph{through alternative official channels}, and compare against
other documents.
\end{goodbox}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{note}{What this result confirms beyond the detection itself}
Two of the corrections made after the first validation are visible in this
output, independently produced by a second reviewer who knew nothing of them.

\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{The narrative no longer contradicts the findings.} The explanation reads
\emph{``Analyse visuelle: …''} then \emph{``Contrôles automatiques: aucun écart
relevé…''}. Each statement is attributed to its stage, and both are true at
once: the model saw the date problem, the deterministic checks correctly found
nothing in their own scope.

\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{The verification step no longer routes through the document.} The
recommendation names \emph{alternative official channels} rather than the
contact details printed on the page --- the precise flaw reported by the first
reviewer.
\end{note}

\vspace{4pt}
\begin{badbox}{The control was not scored}
Document A exists to answer the question the reviewer wrote at the top of both
files: \emph{``Vérifier que DocShield AI ne génère pas de faux positifs.''}
It was uploaded, but the captured screen shows it before the analysis was run,
so \textbf{no result is recorded for the control}.

\vspace{3pt}
Detection on B is therefore established; the absence of a false positive on A is
not. A test that only ever fires proves nothing on its own --- the control is
what separates a detector from an alarm that is always on. Running Document A is
the single most useful next step in this dossier.
\end{badbox}

\begin{note}{The reframing, which matters more than any single item}
\emph{``Ne pas seulement analyser si le document a l'air correct, mais aider une
organisation à sécuriser une décision réelle''} --- awarding a contract, granting
credit, hiring, paying a supplier, settling a claim.

\vspace{3pt}
That moves the unit of value from the document to the decision it supports. Six
of the eight proposals follow from it: they are not detection features, they are
what a reviewer needs before signing. It also explains why external sources
recur --- a decision cannot be secured by a document vouching for itself.
\end{note}

\vspace{4pt}
\fld{On the interface} The reviewer would replace any wording suggesting
\emph{authentic} with \emph{low risk / to be checked / high risk}, plus the
precise reason for the flag. The risk badge already reads that way; the
recommendation label \emph{``Document acceptable''} does not, and still implies
a verdict on authenticity. Small change, and directly on the boundary all three
reviewers have now named.

\vspace{5pt}
\begin{note}{What three independent reviews converge on}
An HR practitioner, a company director and a project manager, none aware of the
others' feedback, arrived at the same boundary from three directions: the score
must be trustworthy, detection must stay distinct from proof, and the interface
must never say \emph{authentic}.

\vspace{3pt}
No reviewer asked for more detection categories. All three asked for the product
to be honest about what it does not establish. That is the clearest product
signal the challenge produced.
\end{note}

\heading{7.}{Summary and evidence checklist}

\fld{Completed} Three external companies, three reviewers --- an HR
practitioner, a company director and a project manager --- seven documents
tested, four defects found and four corrections shipped within the challenge
window.

\fld{Confirmed from outside} The boundary the product was built on --- report
anomalies, never certify authenticity --- was reached independently by all three
reviewers, none of them told it and none aware of the others. Two of the
corrections made after the first validation are visible in the second reviewer's
test output. No reviewer asked for more detection categories.

\fld{Detection demonstrated} On a falsification designed by a third party, the
prototype isolated the planted defect, named it correctly and localised it on
the document.

\fld{Not yet demonstrated} The absence of false positives. The control document
of that same test was never scored, and the two-page invoice from the first
validation has not been re-run.

\fld{Next step} Run Document A, the control. It is the cheapest test remaining
and the only one that turns the current evidence into a complete result.

\vspace{6pt}
\begin{note}{Evidence checklist}
\yes Three companies contacted, all three replied with written feedback\\
\yes All three messages captured and reproduced verbatim\\
\yes Root cause identified in code for all four defects\\
\yes Corrections implemented and deployed, published in the project repository\\
\yes Detection confirmed on a third party's falsified document\\
\yes Eight product proposals recorded against the current state\\
\no Control document not scored --- absence of false positives unverified\\
\no Retest of the two-page invoice with the corrected prototype
\end{note}
